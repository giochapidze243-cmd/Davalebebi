Birth of HTML

HTML was invented by Tim Berners-Lee between 1989 and 1991.

While he first proposed the concept of the World Wide Web and a global hypertext system at 'European Organization for Nuclear Research' in 1989 the language itself was formally developed and publicly introduced in late 1991 with his document titled 'HTML Tags'
